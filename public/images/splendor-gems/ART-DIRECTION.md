# Splendor gem family

Technical frame: existing React DOM/CSS game; external SVG at 64×64 viewBox, transparent background, center pivot. Display at 16px card labels, 22px treasury/flight, 38px bank gem. No labels or numbers baked into assets. Six individually addressable SVGs avoid atlas crop/bleed and keep runtime markup at two elements per icon.

Direction selected by delegated Sol review: jewel facets and visible lower bevel, quiet Renaissance table palette, midnight teal field and ivory rails. White diamond, blue six-sided sapphire, emerald step-cut, ruby octagon, dark onyx and hexagonal gold retain separate silhouettes and names. Shared upper-left soft light and lower-right dark facet. The white diamond is the representative seed; all six use the same 64px canvas and bevel treatment.

Provenance: source-authored SVG geometry by Codex Sol, 2026-10-11, no image generation and no copied external asset. Source SHA-256 hashes live in manifest.json. The canonical development and noble images are never edited or overpainted by this work.

Acceptance: XML/viewBox/path/hash checks and transparent standalone SVG; native 16/22/38px on ivory/teal, grayscale distinction, selected/focus/disabled presentation and original-card readability require browser inspection. Initial manifest status deliberately records the pending visual checks.
