# Marble reference decisions

The user requested commercial Blue Marble / Korean Everybody's Marble references and rejected the initial 18-space variant. Root inspected the classic board photograph and photographed manual pages, plus Netmarble's map-guide diagrams. The chosen replacement uses 40 spaces and six Golden Key spaces, with the couple's city choices Gwangju and Santo Domingo.

## Evidence inspected

- Manufacturer product pages: https://www.bluemarble1982.com/boardgame and https://www.bluemarble1982.com/21.
- A reader-hosted scan of the printed C-MECCA/씨앗사 manual and board photograph: https://m.blog.naver.com/captjangedu/223024768309. Downloaded review copies are in `/tmp/naver-manual-images/`. Root inspected board photo `04.png`, setup `05.png`, building/end rules `07.png`, supplementary rules `08.png`, Golden Key/travel `09.png`, and island/social fund `10.png`.
- Netmarble's official map guide: https://modoo.netmarble.net/Guide/Contents.asp?id=gmm&depth1=325&depth2=1494&depth3=3081&title= . Root inspected the downloaded diagram montage `/tmp/netmarble-maprefs/montage.jpg`, showing Korea-map train travel, world-map island, and distinct space-map mechanisms.

The classic reference uses a circulating Golden Key deck, three missed island turns with doubles escape, a social-fund deposit and collection mechanism, destination selection for travel without dice on the next turn, and distinct villa/building/hotel constructions. Time-limited victory compares assets. Netmarble map rules vary, and its Korea-map train should not be represented as an original classic-board rule.

## Private adaptation

The app combines the classic 40-space structure and Golden Key / island / fund mechanisms with illustrated train travel, personalized cities and two-player costs. Building upgrades are simplified to three sequential levels and can be purchased on one's own city; it does not reproduce the classic first/second-half schedule or publisher card economics. Funds, travel actions, escape passes, costs and destination resolution are authoritative game state. Existing unversioned 18-space rooms retain their old board and mechanics; new version-2 rooms use the replacement board.

No exact publisher clone or original-edition rule parity is claimed. Reference diagrams are review material, not assets rendered by the app.
